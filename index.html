Welcome to the Studio

The LGU Music Society is a student-run community at Lahore Garrison University for singers, instrumentalists, songwriters, producers and everyone who simply loves music. We rehearse weekly, record in our campus studio and perform at university events throughout the year.

Whether you have played for years or have never touched an instrument, there is a place for you in our society.

What We Do

Our main activities this semester:

  • Weekly Jam Sessions – open rehearsals every Thursday afternoon.
  • Studio Recording Workshops – learn to record and mix your own tracks.
  • Vocal Training – breathing, pitch and stage-presence practice.
  • Songwriting Circle – write and share original Urdu and English songs.
  • Annual Music Night – a full concert performed by our members.
