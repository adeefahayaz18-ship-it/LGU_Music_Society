Annual Music Night 2026

Date: Friday, 18 December 2026  |  Venue: LGU Main Auditorium  |  Time: 6:00 PM

Music Night is the biggest event in the society's calendar. Over three hours, members perform solo vocals, bands, qawwali, classical instrumentals and original compositions in front of students, faculty and invited guests.

Auditions are held two weeks before the show, and every registered member is welcome to try out. Our studio team will record the full concert and share the best tracks on the society's page.

Announcement

Tip: Early-bird members who register before 30 November get free access to the recording workshop. Bring your own instrument to Thursday jam sessions!
